About

Daniel began his career as a trader and has spent more than a decade in capital markets. He has worked directly with chairs and CEOs at Nasdaq- and NYSE-listed companies in fintech, digital assets, bitcoin mining and AI infrastructure, running investor relations, earnings, annual meetings, financings and listings.

When a company prepares to go public, he brings in the full professional team: banks, lawyers and auditors.

He advises through 58th Street Digital, a New York practice where he works with a small team. The team also builds its own research tools, including Alfred, an agent that answers investor questions from company filings, and SmallCap Signal.

Bylines in CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Entrepreneur and CoinsPaid Media. DACFP digital assets certification.
